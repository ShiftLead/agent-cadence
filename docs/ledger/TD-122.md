## TD-122: The one-sentence rule for what waits on the person in cadence §3.3 and the stage briefs, and a cadence-check warning on an entry with no `Ask:` line

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-19 (ShiftLead's designer, from shiftlead#TD-909's design)
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open — waits for shiftlead#TD-772's read-back of the Queue's trial (2026-10-30), since a rule on how the person is asked changes what that trial measures (shiftlead ADR 2026-10-19 §8)
**Blocked by:** shiftlead#TD-772
**Location:** `files/docs/cadence.md` §3.3 (a board line's shape), the stage briefs and presets the cadence ships (`files/docs/procedures/`, the briefs synced to each repo), `files/scripts/check_cadence.py` (the PR's cadence row)

**Why:** shiftlead#TD-909 (design §4.10 *An item leads with one sentence*, *The home warns, never refuses*): the rule is enforced mechanically, never by a seat — Paul, 2026-10-19: *less desirable would be having the techlead monitor new items added to the queue for clarity*.

**Fix:** (1) cadence §3.3 and the briefs: what waits on the person — an `ask` or `steer` to the person, a board line, a ledger entry — leads with one sentence that states the decision or the task, then what each answer leads to (`<label> (default) — <consequence>`), then the context; an open question with no default stays legitimate. (2) `check_cadence.py` **warns, never fails**, on a PR that adds or edits an entry with `Owner: paul`, `decision (paul)` or `Answers:` and no `Ask:` line in its body, naming the entry. Tests: the warning fires on an added entry and an edited one, not on an archived one, and never turns the row red.

**Done when** cadence §3.3 and the shipped briefs say the rule and the cadence check warns on an entry with no `Ask:` line.
