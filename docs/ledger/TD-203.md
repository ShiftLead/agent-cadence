## TD-203: Name the reviewers §4.8 already asks for and bound their rounds: `review-model` takes an ordered list, the fact-check gets its own `fact-check-model`, and design review rounds get a cap, a stop rule and a findings record

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-10-29 (Paul, session `sl-tds`: "file it with the ordered list, maybe have the fact check model defined separately")
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open. Design decided 2026-10-30 (Paul: "go"; ADR `docs/plans/2026-10-30-design-review-rounds.md`, six review rounds); ready to build.
**Taken by:** b675de241e6c
**Location:** `files/scripts/cadence_config.py` (`KEYS`, `COUNT_KEYS`, `get`: `review-model` as a list, `fact-check-model`, `design-rounds`, `design-rounds-wide`); `files/docs/cadence.md` §4.8 (the reviewers and the *Design review rounds* paragraph), §9.18 (the keys), Adoption checklist step 10; `files/docs/procedures/cadence.md` step 3; `files/docs/adr-template.md` (an optional *Review record*); the seed `files/docs/cadence.toml`; `files/docs/cadence-changes.md`; `tests/test_cadence_config.sh`, `tests/test_adopt.sh`; ShiftLead's readers of `review-model` (its techlead review, TD-857's fact-checker seat), as a shiftlead entry

**Why:** §4.8 already describes more than one reviewer: "Iterated rounds with more than one reviewer model run one at a time, cheaper first", and doc-only PRs get "a fact-check against the repo". But the settings name one model, `review-model` (default `sonnet`). The second reviewer, and the order the rounds go in, live only in how a person and their sessions happen to work. In this repo that has meant Sonnet fact-checking against the code and Fable reviewing the design, run by hand and once started in parallel by mistake (2026-10-29, the TD-173 plan's reviews). A rule whose inputs aren't configured gets followed from memory.

The fact-check is a different job from a review round. It reads a diff and the files it cites, and checks each claim; it makes no judgement on design. ShiftLead's TD-857 is moving it to a Gemini CLI seat on a free tier, naming the reviewer `<adapter>/<model>`. So its model is chosen on different grounds than a design reviewer's, and may not be a Claude model at all.

**Fix:** as the ADR's Design section (rules 1–11) states; in short:
- **`review-model` is an ordered list** (`"sonnet, fable"`); a one-value setting behaves as today, `get review-model --first` prints the round-one model, and the env override takes a list too. A **round** is one pass through the list, its reviews one at a time, each review's fixes applied before the next reads the plan.
- **`fact-check-model`** (a model or `<adapter>/<model>`, falling back to the first `review-model`) runs the fact-checks: the whole plan before round 1, only what changed since the last fact-check before each later round, and a closing fact-check after the last round; each repeats fix-then-check until clean and is not counted as a review.
- **`design-rounds`** (default 4) and **`design-rounds-wide`** (default 6) cap the rounds; *wide* is a change to another repo, to what consumers run from the payload, to a vendored or published format, or something irreversible, stated before round 1.
- **Labels and record:** each finding is `build-changing` (two builders would build different things, or something stated is false) or `minor`, and the label stands; every finding is a row of the plan's findings table (id, round, by, topic, finding, class, status: `applied` | `rejected: <why>` | `open` | `reopens F<n>`).
- **How the rounds end:** the stop rule (a round whose reviews raise nothing build-changing, the cap's last round included), the cap (its last round still raised one), or after a bought round. A *recurring topic* (a review's build-changing finding reopening an earlier one or following that topic's fix, in two or more rounds) is named, never an ending.
- **The end-of-rounds item, only when needed:** at the cap or after a bought round, with a recurring topic, with a build-changing closing finding, or where the repo's own rules require a go. `Answers: Go | One more round | Hold: <note>`; `Hold` keeps the block and puts the note in the entry's `**Ask:**`. Otherwise the unreviewed fixes go into the build entries' Fix and the session files the builds.
- **The text in cadence.md names no personal rule** (the ADR's plan note stays in the ADR). §4.8 gains the paragraph, §9.18 the four keys, the Adoption checklist step 10 names them, the procedure's step 3 asks for each round from them, `adr-template.md` gains an optional *Review record*, and one `cadence-changes.md` entry announces it.
- **ShiftLead:** file a shiftlead entry (its id from ShiftLead's counter): its techlead review and TD-857's fact-checker seat read these keys.
- **Tests** (`tests/test_cadence_config.sh`): a list parses and `--first` reads its head; a single value is unchanged; `fact-check-model` falls back; the env override takes a list; a malformed list is an error naming the key; both round keys `get` their defaults and a repo file's value; a non-integer or zero value is a `--check` error; the parity check covers the seed's new lines.

**Done when** a repo can name its reviewers in order, its fact-checker, and its two round caps in `docs/cadence.toml`; §4.8 states the design-rounds rules and §9.18 the keys; the procedure asks for each round from them; a `cadence-changes.md` entry announces it; and the shiftlead entry is filed.
