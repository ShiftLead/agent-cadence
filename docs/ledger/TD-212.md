## TD-212: An interactive design holds its builds only in Paul's layer: make decision (<person>) on an in-conversation design the cadence rule for every user

**Priority:** Medium
**Added:** 2026-10-31 (Paul, session led-tds: "make sure this is generalized for other user names")
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Location:** cadence.md §2.4; files/scripts/ledger.py (new); Paul's layer §2.4 add

**Why:** Paul's personal layer carries the rule that makes an interactive design hold its builds: an entry filed while its design conversation is still going lands with `**Blocked by:** decision (Paul)`, and its builds stay blocked until the review rounds converge and he says go (layer §2.4 add, harvested 2026-10-26). It exists in one person's layer, with one person's name, so another user's interactive session files its design entries unblocked and an unattended session can pick them mid-conversation. On 2026-10-31 Paul decided (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md` §3) that this decision gate replaces ShiftLead's temporary Hold, which is being removed (filed on shiftlead), and accepted the short window between adding a decision to an existing entry by PR and that PR's merge. The rule is general, so it belongs in `cadence.md`, not in a layer.

**Fix:** `cadence.md` §2.4 gains the rule for every user: an entry an interactive session files while its design is still in conversation starts with `Blocked by: decision (<person>)`, where `<person>` is the human driving that session, never a hard-coded name, with an `**Ask:**` saying what releases it (for example "Go once the shape is final?"). Its builds are filed blocked the same way until the person's go. The person's Go on the entry's board `decide` item lifts it (ledgerboard TD-010's decide lift; until that lands, a session lifts it with `ledger.py set`). `ledger.py new` and the session-start text point at the rule. Paul's layer then drops its copy.

**Done when:** §2.4 states the rule with `<person>`, `ledger.py new` points at it, and Paul's layer no longer carries it.
