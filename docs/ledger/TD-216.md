## TD-216: ledger.py becomes a wrapper over ledgerboard's ledger.py, keeping the counter, the roster resolver and the git date lookup

**Priority:** Medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-04
**Status:** Open
**Taken by:** 2e9dce7d0dea
**Blocked by:** TD-215
**Part of:** TD-214
**Location:** files/scripts/ledger.py

**Why:** Two ledger writers drift (ledgerboard TD-033's reason). ledgerboard's target design (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md`) §1.1 makes ledgerboard's `ledger.py` the writer and each consumer's a thin wrapper.

**Fix:** Per ledgerboard's target design (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md`) §1.1 and §7.2 step 3:
- `ledger.py` calls ledgerboard's `ledger.py` for every read and write: gates, `archive --dropped`, `set --override`, `migrate` over the module's pure `migrate_entry`;
- it keeps only the I/O the package must not hold: the id counter, the roster resolver, and the git date lookup, which it passes in;
- the writers' dates switch to UTC (`datetime.now(timezone.utc).date()`);
- exit codes follow the package's table (0 ok, 1 refused, 2 usage, 3 needs, 4 write failed), and callers that read them are updated.

**Done when:** `ledger.py` holds no parsing or writing of its own, its dates are UTC, and the suite and the cadence check pass.
