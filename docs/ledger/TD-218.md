**Location:** files/scripts/check_cadence.py; files/scripts/board_edit.py; files/scripts/ledger.py (set by=); files/scripts/forge.py

**Why:** An answered decision does not lift its gate today, and nothing stops a PR from lifting one without the person's answer. v2.4 ships the verdicts; the gate must call them (ledgerboard's target design (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md`) §1.1, §1.2).

**Fix:** Per ledgerboard's target design (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md`) §1.1, §1.2 and §7.2 step 3, in one PR that turns the tripwire on:
- the `set by=` wrapper sequence (§1.1), with a fixture for each suffix;
- the decide lift through ledgerboard's `board.py decide --by`, written as one lift PR;
- `check_cadence.py`'s carve-out widened to accept that shape by calling `lift_pr_verdict`, with `tripwire` and `uncovered_decisions` on every PR;
- the merge-gate check that each new decision has a board item;
- `forge.py` adds `createdAt` to `PR_FIELDS` and the neutral PR record, so the gate can check a lift's date.

**Done when:** A decide answers and lifts in one PR the gate accepts; a lift without an answer fails; the suite and the cadence check pass.
