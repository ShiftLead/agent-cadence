**Location:** files/scripts/ (event runner, sweep); the session-start hook

**Why:** v2.4's event gates (pr-merged, file-contains, ref-includes) and the gate sweep are pure functions; something must fetch the facts and write the lifts (ledgerboard's target design (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md`) §1.2, §4).

**Fix:** Per ledgerboard's target design (ShiftLead/ledgerboard `docs/plans/target-design.md`) §1.2, §4 and §7.2 step 3:
- an event runner that resolves each open event gate's facts (a PR's merge, a file's contents, a ref's history) with the argument confinement §4 sets, and lifts what fired through `ledger.py lift`;
- the gate sweep's I/O around the module's `sweep()`: what it reads, when it runs, and how its findings reach the person.
- the sweep's I/O passes each roster board's origin URL to `sweep()`, which orders boards by `origin_key` itself (the person's-board pick), and the ids it files name the repo by its lowercased `origin_path` (target design §3.2; moved from TD-217, techlead mail m-43491d0fef64).

**Done when:** Event gates lift when their event happens, the sweep runs and reports, with tests, and the suite passes.
