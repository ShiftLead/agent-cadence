## TD-243: A design's rounds end with a build-changing fix that no review has read: cadence.md §4.8's stop rule and cap end them, and the end-of-rounds item can default to Go

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (Paul, through his session ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 1cf71af5dbed43d5a592caf2559442f2
**Blocked by:** decision (Paul)
**Location:** files/docs/cadence.md §4.8 (Design review rounds: the stop rule, the cap, the closing fact-check, the end-of-rounds item); files/docs/procedures/cadence.md step 3; docs/plans/2026-10-30-design-review-rounds.md (the decided ADR); files/docs/cadence-changes.md; files/scripts/review_stats.py

**Why:** A design's review rounds can end, and reach the person, while a build-changing fix has not been read by any review. cadence.md §4.8 (*Design review rounds*) ends the rounds at the **cap** even when the cap's last round raised a build-changing finding, so that finding's fix is applied and then read by nobody. It also says a **closing fact-check**'s findings are *applied, never reviewed*. The end-of-rounds item lists *the findings no review has read*, but nothing makes the author's recommendation follow from them. So the item's default can be `Go` while a fix that changes what gets built is unread. The person then has to press `One more round` themselves, and that buys a whole round (every review re-reads the whole plan), not a read of the fixes alone.

Seen 2026-11-06 on ShiftLead (`docs/reviews/` there):
- TD-1434 ended at the cap (4). Round 4's F55 and F56, build-changing, are recorded *applied (fixed, not reviewed)*. F54, raised by the round's first review (R11), was fixed before its second (R12) read the plan.
- TD-1437 ended at the cap (4). Round 4's F51 is recorded *applied (fixed, not reviewed)*.
- TD-1435 ended by the stop rule in round 2. Its closing fact-check raised F41, build-changing, applied after the last review. Its record says the author recommended `One more round` there, as §4.8 already asks for a closing fact-check that changes the design.

The person chose `One more round` six times that day across designs. What prompted it was a rule in one session's memory (*recommend one more round while any build-changing fix is unread*), not §4.8. That rule lives in no consumer's cadence, so the next design session will not apply it.

The harm: a build is released on text that two builders could read two ways, which is the very thing the `build-changing` label marks. The person is left to notice it item by item, and the only answer on offer is the costliest one.

**Ask:** Write this design in a session with you and agree its shape (the closing read, the cap, the item's line), then run its review rounds? It changes §4.8 for every consumer, so it waits for your go, not a grinder's pick.

**Fix:** A design (this rule is wide: it changes §4.8 for every consumer, and ShiftLead's flow and designer brief follow it). The asker's shape, for the design round to test:

1. **The rounds do not end while a build-changing fix is unread.** Neither the stop rule nor the cap ends them while any build-changing finding stands `applied` and no review has read it since. This covers a cap round's findings and a closing fact-check's. Instead, one **closing read** runs: a targeted review of only those fixes (the diff since the last review that read the plan, with the findings it answers). It runs once, then the item, or the builds, follow as now.
2. **The cap bounds full rounds, not the closing read.** `design-rounds` / `design-rounds-wide` keep counting whole-plan rounds; the closing read is outside the count, as the fact-checks already are.
3. **The end-of-rounds item says which it is**: *every build-changing fix has been read by a review*, or names the findings that have not, each by its `F<n>`. Its recommendation follows from that.
4. **The record**: the closing read is a row of the Reviews table (a role of its own, or `design` under the last round: the design says which). Its findings are rows as any review's.

Open for the design to settle:
- What a closing read that itself raises a build-changing finding does: another closing read, `One more round`, or the item with that finding named.
- Whether the closing fact-check runs before or after the closing read, and which of them reads the other's fixes.
- How *read by a review* is recorded in the findings table (a status such as `applied, read R<n>`), so the item, and `review_stats.py`, can tell.
- The procedure's wording (`files/docs/procedures/cadence.md` step 3), the ADR the rule lives in (`docs/plans/2026-10-30-design-review-rounds.md`, decided: the change is a new ADR or a superseding clause, §7.5), and a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

Then tell ShiftLead's techlead this entry's number, so ShiftLead's flow and designer brief mirror it after its next pin bump.

**Done when:** cadence.md §4.8 and the procedure say that the rounds do not end while a build-changing fix is unread, and say what the closing read is. The end-of-rounds item states that every build-changing fix was read, or names those that were not. The decision is recorded as §7.5 asks for a decided ADR. cadence-changes.md has the line.
