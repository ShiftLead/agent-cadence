## TD-247: A minor-only fact-check repeats until it raises nothing, and the review record's tallies are kept by hand, so seats loop 3–4 times over wording

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-06 (Paul, through his session ao-led-tds-ledger, from a trial review of ShiftLead seats' transcripts; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Owner:** person
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** eccf5528c1524b908b772b91192ecaa4
**Location:** files/docs/cadence.md §4.8 (fact-checks), §4.9 (the verdict line); files/docs/procedures/cadence.md step 3; files/scripts/review_stats.py

**Why:** A fact-check that raises only minor findings repeats until it raises nothing, so seats loop over wording. cadence.md §4.8 (*Design review rounds*) says each fact-check "repeats fix-then-check until it raises nothing", and that includes the closing fact-check. In the trial review (ShiftLead seats, 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-06), a closing fact-check that was SHIP with minor findings was treated as BLOCK. The seat fixed it and ran the check again 3–4 times over wording and over hand-kept tallies in the review record that had gone stale (4+ PRs: ShiftLead #2911, #2991, #3035, #3039).

Two costs: each re-run is a model review's tokens and a head move (which, before TD-245, means another precheck). The counts in `docs/reviews/TD-NNN.md`, such as build-changing findings per round, are kept by hand even though `files/scripts/review_stats.py` already parses the tables. So each fix to a finding makes a tally stale, and the next check finds it.

Related to TD-243, which is the opposite case: a build-changing fix that no review reads. This entry is about minor fixes read too often. A design that settles one should check the other.

**Ask:** Agree the shape (one re-run for minor-only findings, over the changed lines; SHIP-with-minors is not a BLOCK; generated tallies), with TD-243's design if it is open, then build it? It changes §4.8 and §4.9 for every consumer, so it waits for your go, not a grinder's pick.

**Fix:** A proposal for §4.8 and the procedure:
1. **Minor-only findings get one re-run at most.** A fact-check (or a PR's docs fact-check, §4.9) whose findings are all `minor` is fixed and then re-checked once, over the changed lines only. A second minor-only result is applied and recorded, never re-run. A `build-changing` finding still repeats until it is gone.
2. **Say what the verdict means.** `SHIP` with minor findings means the minors are fixed and then merged. It is not a BLOCK. Write that in §4.9 beside the verdict line, so a seat does not read it as one.
3. **Generate the counts.** `review_stats.py` (or a `--write` mode) generates the review record's per-round tallies from its tables, so nobody keeps a count by hand. A fact-check then reads the generated counts and does not judge them.
This changes §4.8 for every consumer, so it needs a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line and, like TD-243, Paul's go on the shape. Settle it alongside TD-243 if that design is open.

**Done when:** cadence.md §4.8 and §4.9 say a minor-only fact-check gets at most one re-run over the changed lines, and that SHIP with minor findings is not a BLOCK. The review record's tallies are generated, not hand-kept. cadence-changes.md has the line.
