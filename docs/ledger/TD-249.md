**Why:** When a design's review rounds end, the person decides from the end-of-rounds item (cadence.md §4.8), and that item and the review record it points to are written at the level of findings: `F<n>` ids, mechanisms, file and section refs. Nothing in the cadence gives the person a plain-words account of what the design now says, so to judge `Go | One more round | Hold` they either read the whole plan or decide from the finding-level record. Neither is what the item is for.

ShiftLead has already met this and designed the cure for its own designs: TD-1527 (shiftlead#3203, merged 2026-11-07, `docs/design/4.9c-flows.md` *The designer writes the synopsis*). Each design keeps `docs/synopses/TD-<n>.md`, written last by every design PR after the round's fixes and rewritten whole each round, in four sections (*What it does*, *The key choices, and why*, *What it rules out*, *What you will see*), at most 40 non-blank lines, in plain words with no finding ids, code or file refs, and read by the closing fact-check against the design. Its design leaves the cadence's own part to this ledger: that a design's end-of-rounds item links its synopsis in every repo. Until §4.8 says so, a consumer's design sessions other than ShiftLead's designer write none, and ShiftLead's item links a file the cadence it runs does not name.

Asked by ShiftLead's designer (`ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1`, about shiftlead#TD-1527) on 2026-11-07.

**Ask:** Design this with you (the synopsis's place, shape and check, and whether it rides with TD-243's §4.8 change), then run its review rounds? It changes §4.8 for every consumer, so it waits for your go, not a grinder's pick.

**Fix:** A feature: its design round settles the shape. The asker's proposal, for that round to test:

1. cadence.md §4.8 *Design review rounds*: a design keeps a **synopsis** for the person, written by each round's PR last (after its fixes) and rewritten whole, in plain words with no finding ids, code or file refs, short enough to read on one screen; the **closing fact-check** reads it against the design (it says nothing the design does not, and leaves out none of the choices the person would weigh).
2. **The end-of-rounds item** links the synopsis (in its detail, beside the review record), so the person judges from it; the item and the record keep their finding-level words and do not repeat it.

Open for the design to settle:
- Where the synopsis lives in a consumer (ShiftLead's `docs/synopses/TD-<n>.md`, or beside the review record, e.g. a section of `docs/reviews/TD-NNN.md`), and whether the cadence fixes the four sections and the 40-line cap or leaves them to the repo.
- Whether a check holds it (a `ledger`/`board` row, or a repo test as ShiftLead's), or the closing fact-check alone.
- How it sits with TD-243, which also changes §4.8's end of rounds and its item: one design for both, or this one after it.
- The procedure's wording (`files/docs/procedures/cadence.md` step 3), the ADR (`docs/plans/2026-10-30-design-review-rounds.md` is decided: a new ADR or a superseding clause, §7.5), and a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

Then tell ShiftLead's designer (or techlead) the result, so TD-1527's Log names it and ShiftLead's brief follows after its next pin bump.

**Done when:** cadence.md §4.8 and the procedure say a design keeps a plain-words synopsis that the closing fact-check reads and the end-of-rounds item links. The decision is recorded as §7.5 asks for a decided ADR, and cadence-changes.md has the line.
