## TD-254: board_edit.py decide and reply write a form answer as written (Decided: Hold: <note>) instead of filling it, as ShiftLead's board does

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 4484db3aebd342b3baf0b1f7628093ed

**Why:** A session that records the person's answer with `board_edit.py decide` cannot fill a form answer the way ShiftLead's own board does. A form answer is an offered answer that needs the person's words, such as `Hold: <note>`. ShiftLead's TD-1557 design (its design PR ShiftLead/shiftlead#3511) settles that a form is filled, never pressed as written: words that begin with the form's head (any case) fill it, written `<head>: <words>` on one line, and a form sent as written or with only its placeholder is refused with `<head> needs its words`. ShiftLead's `_board_answer` does this through ledgerboard's general answer form (`is_form`, `form_head`, `fill_form(said, offered, form=)`), which ledgerboard is to release.

`board_edit.py decide` today hands `--answer` to ledgerboard's `plan_edit()` as free text (`files/scripts/board_edit.py` main(), ~535), and `reply` appends its `--text` as a reply. So a session or a person's terminal that answers `Hold: <note>` writes `Decided: Hold: <note>` with the placeholder still in it, and words such as `hold until the bump lands` sent with `reply` are written as a reply, not as the form's press, so the item stays open. The same board then reads differently depending on whether ShiftLead's page or a session wrote the answer, and every consumer runs `board_edit.py` from its pin.

Asked for by ShiftLead's designer ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1 (2026-11-12, `ao td add`), for ShiftLead TD-1557. ShiftLead's build TD-1698 waits on the pin bump that brings the ledgerboard release, since that bump also vendors it into ShiftLead.

**Location:** files/scripts/board_edit.py (decide and reply: the docstring ~35–56, main() ~484–560, plan_edit's --answer/--text ~535); files/scripts/ledgerboard/ (vendored, vendor_ledgerboard.sh); files/docs/cadence.md §3.5 (an item's answers)

**Fix:** Once ledgerboard's release with the general answer form is out:

In `board_edit.py decide`, fill a form answer through `ledgerboard.fill_form`, as ShiftLead's `_board_answer` does: words beginning with one of the item's form heads (any case) fill that form, written `<head>: <words>` on one line; a form sent as written or with only its placeholder is refused with `<head> needs its words`, never written as `Decided: Hold: <note>`.
3. In `board_edit.py reply`, on an open `decide` or `look` line, words beginning with a form's head are that form's press: a decide, not a reply.
4. Say it in `board_edit.py`'s docstring and in cadence.md §3.5 where the answers are, with a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

If the release already applies the form inside `plan_edit()`, step 2 shrinks to the vendor and the tests; check the release before writing a wrapper. Read ShiftLead's TD-1557 design as merged, not this entry, for the rule's exact wording: the design PR was still open when this was filed.
