## TD-258: gate_sweep.py gives every until gate one lift branch, so a re-gated entry's earlier closed lift blocks its next one

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12 (ShiftLead's grinder ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-2, through ao td add for ShiftLead TD-1751; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e48a557a2ee4488c9b70f6fe9818b937

**Why:** `gate_sweep.py`'s `lift_branch()` gives every `until` gate on an entry the same branch, `lift/TD-n/until`, while each event gate gets a branch of its own name. `--apply` then reads every PR ever opened on that branch (`be.prs_for_branch(branch)`). So after one `until` lift of an entry is closed unmerged, every later `until` gate on that entry finds the closed PR. The sweep then files an act item, *Lift of until <the new date> on TD-n was closed*, instead of lifting the gate, and that item names a gate no lift was ever opened for. The merged case goes wrong too: after one `until` lift merged, a later `until` on the same entry reads *its PR merged and origin still carries the gate — fetch, then sweep* on every sweep, and is never lifted. Every entry that is re-gated `until` a later day (a re-check, a live check's look) is open to this, in every consumer that runs the sweep from its pin.

Seen live in ShiftLead, whose home sweep uses the same key: its sweep PR ShiftLead/shiftlead#3624 filed the act item for TD-1648's `until 2026-11-13`, whose only closed lift was #3507, a lift of `until 2026-11-12`. ShiftLead fixed its own sweep under its TD-1751 by putting the date in the branch. Asked for by ShiftLead's grinder ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-2 (2026-11-13 UTC, `ao td add --repo agent-cadence`).

**Location:** files/scripts/gate_sweep.py (lift_branch ~253–259, LIFT_BRANCH ~249, the --apply lift loop ~426–473, the docstring ~35); files/docs/cadence.md §2.14 (the lift branch); tests/test_gate_sweep_apply.sh

**Fix:** 1. In `lift_branch()`, key an `until` gate by its date: `lift/TD-n/until-YYYY-MM-DD`, as ShiftLead's home sweep now does (ShiftLead TD-1751), so the two sweeps write the same branch for the same gate.
2. Keep reading a `lift/TD-n/until` PR opened before the change: an open one on the old branch whose diff lifts this gate is already caught by the `dup` check (an open PR whose diff carries `gate lifted: <gate>`); check that it is, and add nothing more unless it is not. A closed or merged one on the old branch is not this gate's unless its diff or title names this date.
3. Update the docstring (the header's `…/until`, `LIFT_BRANCH`'s comment, `lift_branch()`'s) and `files/docs/cadence.md` §2.14 where it names the lift branch, with a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.
4. In `tests/test_gate_sweep_apply.sh`, move the fixtures off `lift/TD-003/until` and add the case: a closed lift of `until D1`, then a reached `until D2` on the same entry, and the sweep opens a lift for D2 instead of filing the act item.

**Done when:** A reached `until` gate on an entry whose earlier `until` lift was closed or merged gets a lift PR on `lift/TD-n/until-<date>`, not an act item or a *fetch, then sweep* line, and a test in `tests/test_gate_sweep_apply.sh` holds it.
