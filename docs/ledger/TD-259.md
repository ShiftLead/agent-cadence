**Why:** ledgerboard v2.8 is tagged (Paul's release, 2026-11-13; ShiftLead/ledgerboard#281, tag `v2.8`) and is the last v2 minor. This repo vendors v2.7 today (`files/scripts/ledgerboard/`, TD-256), and ShiftLead's own sync follows this repo's vendor, so every v2.8 behaviour waits on this PR: ShiftLead TD-1557's form answers among them (ShiftLead's build TD-1698 waits on the pin bump that brings it).

What v2.8 changes that this repo meets (ledgerboard `SPEC.md`, its v2.8 paragraphs): `set` takes a title and refuses misplaced names (a field `Title` or `Log`; ledgerboard TD-079/080); a `Doer:` field on an act line, written by `board.py add --doer` (TD-083); an offered answer whose label is a form (`<head>:` or `<head>: <…>`) is filled by the person's words through `fill_form`, and a filled form listed in `Lifts:` lifts (TD-084); `ledger.py note` on an archived entry (TD-088); and `migrate` rules 5–8, which move a header `Taken by`, an archived `Waits`, a header `Resolved` and the old decision form (TD-089). Rules 5–8 may find lines in this ledger and its archive that v2.8's `check()` rejects, so vendoring without running `migrate` can turn the cadence check's `ledger` row red on every PR, as TD-256 found for v2.7.

`board_edit.py add` has no `--doer` (`files/scripts/board_edit.py`, the add parser ~180–205), so a session cannot write the `Doer:` field v2.8 defines, and the board would read differently by who wrote it. TD-254 (form answers through `decide` and `reply`) is the other half the asker wants in the same PR.

Asked for by a session of Paul's in ShiftLead/ledgerboard (ao-led-tds-ledger, by `ask`, 2026-11-13).
