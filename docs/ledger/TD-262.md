## TD-262: The cadence check has no carve-out for ShiftLead's lift PR, so a Lift the hold press reads as merged outside step 5 (ShiftLead TD-1829)

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-14 (ShiftLead's designer ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1, through an ask, for ShiftLead TD-1829; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Open

**Blocked by:** event (shiftlead-lift-press-designed)

**Why:** ShiftLead's **Lift the hold** press is about to lift a person's `decision (<p>)` gate itself, in a PR of its own, and this repo's cadence check has no carve-out for that PR. So each such PR fails its review row, and `check_cadence.py --since` lists it under *merged outside step 5*, when it is a tool merge on the person's press. A weekly read that lists the person's own presses as bypasses teaches its reader to skip the list, which then hides a real bypass.

ShiftLead's TD-1829 design (ShiftLead/shiftlead PR #3820, open at 2026-11-14; build ShiftLead TD-1831) has the home lift the gate on the press, since no unattended session may (`agent_sessions.person_session_refusal`, TD-218). The press lands, through ledgerboard's `plan_lift`, one commit whose diff is the lift alone: the entry's `decision (<p>)` removed and a `gate lifted: decision (<p>) — by <p>; answer "…"` Log line, plus any board item that covered the gate closed `— lifted.`. Its subject and PR title are `shiftlead: lift decision (<p>) on TD-n (session n/a)`, and it squash-merges at once, as §4.5's board edits do. ShiftLead's designer asked for this entry (ao-shiftlead-designer-ao-1, 2026-11-14, m-417905155087). The design says the weekly read will list these PRs, accepted, until this lands (TD-1831 Fix step 5).

The carve-outs today don't cover it. `row_lift_pr` (the decide lift, §4.6, TD-218) needs a board item that *gains* a `Decided:` in the PR. Here the item was decided earlier (`go ahead`) or there is none. `row_board_edit_pr` and the board-append PR (TD-237) accept the board alone. The ledger file in this diff sends the PR to ordinary review.

As the asker reported it, and read on the design's open branch only: the subject string, and the diff being the `plan_lift` result alone, with no other file.

**Event (shiftlead-lift-press-designed):** ShiftLead's TD-1829 design (ShiftLead/shiftlead PR #3820) is merged, so the lift PR's subject and diff shape are settled.

**Location:** files/scripts/check_cadence.py (row_lift_pr ~2459, row_board_edit_pr ~2535, merged_outside ~2681); files/scripts/ledgerboard/__init__.py (EDIT_SUBJECT_RE ~2803, plan_lift via ledger.py ~527, lift_pr_verdict ~5752, tripwire ~5594); files/docs/cadence.md §4.4, §4.6; files/docs/cadence-changes.md; tests/test_lift_pr.sh

**Fix:** Add a **lift** carve-out to §4.6, beside the decide lift and TD-237's board append. Judge it by a verdict that *recomputes* the lift, never by the title alone, since a title-only pass would let any hand edit through as the person's press.

1. **The verdict belongs to ledgerboard**, as `lift_pr_verdict()` does (ledgerboard TD-032). Ask ledgerboard for it with `ao td add --repo ledgerboard` (cadence.md §3.7). It would add a `lift` action to the subject grammar beside `EDIT_SUBJECT_RE`, and a pure verdict over base and head trees that passes a PR only when all of these hold: one commit under that subject; the changed ledger file is byte-equal to `plan_lift(base, TD-n, "decision (<p>)", <p>, answer=…)`'s result, with its Log date within a day of the PR's opening; the board changes at most the one item that covered the gate at the base, closed `— lifted.` as `edit_line()` writes it, or not at all; no other path. A tripwire flag on the same trees sends it to review. Whether a lift with no covering board item may pass on its Log line alone is that design's question. The Log line is the only trace of the press there, and the tripwire calls it forgeable.
2. **Wire it here** once the release carrying it is vendored. Add a `row_lift_only_pr` (or widen `row_lift_pr`) in `files/scripts/check_cadence.py` that sets `carve_out: "lift"`, so `merged_outside()` lists it as a tool merge. Write §4.6's sentence in `files/docs/cadence.md` (and §4.4's cross-reference), add a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` entry, and add a case to `tests/test_lift_pr.sh`, beside the decide lift's cases, for: a lift that passes, a lift that also edits another line, and a subject without a recomputing diff.

**Done when:** `check_cadence.py --pr <n>` passes the review row of a ShiftLead lift PR (one commit, subject `shiftlead: lift decision (<p>) on TD-n (session n/a)`, its diff exactly `plan_lift`'s result plus at most its covering item closed `— lifted.`) with `carve_out: "lift"`, and `--since` lists it as a tool merge. It sends the same subject with any other change to ordinary review. cadence.md §4.6 says so, and a test holds each case.
