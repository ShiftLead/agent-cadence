## TD-279: The board report suite fails every Monday, when the nudge adds its weekly review

**Priority:** High
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-16
**Owner:** grinder
**Kind:** build
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** 3f209e5cf15e4b50be1d08e862d1e0d7

**Why:** `tests/test_p2_report.sh`'s check *v3 board: nudge mode lists the due item* runs the nudge with no `--force-weekly` and asserts that the not-yet-due item is absent from its output. The nudge appends its weekly review of every open item when `today.weekday() == 0` (`nudge_user_attention.py`, `main`), so on every Monday the item appears there and the suite fails. The precheck is the merge gate, so nothing in this repo can merge on a Monday (found 2026-11-16, failing the precheck of the lift PRs #234 and #235).

**Location:** tests/test_p2_report.sh

**Fix:** Assert the absence only in the due list, cutting the output at the weekly review's header before the `! grep`, so the check holds on any weekday. The presence check is unchanged.

**Done when:** `tests/test_p2_report.sh` passes on a Monday, as the precheck on 2026-11-16 shows.
