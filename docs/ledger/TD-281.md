## TD-281: How a design's review rounds end is judged card by card: write the guidance down, and let each repo say which designs are far-reaching

**Priority:** High
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-16 (Paul, through his session ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Owner:** person
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d56a567618434ac5931a3a6056b45e29
**Blocked by:** decision (Paul)

**Why:** cadence.md §4.8's stop rule and cap end a design's review rounds, and the end-of-rounds item then defaults to *One more round*, or to *Go* at the cap. Nothing in it weighs whether the build-changing findings are shrinking, whether the fixes no review has read change the build or only the wording, or whether the same concern keeps coming back. So the item's recommendation is often not the one a careful reader would make, and the person has to judge it card by card.

During ledgerboard's v3 migration (2026-11-04 to 2026-11-16), an interactive session (`led-tds`) judged exactly those things on about 175 end-of-rounds cards. It disagreed with the card's own recommendation 41 times (23%), almost all on how a design's rounds should end, and Paul sided with it 31 times. That judgement can be written down and applied the same way in every repo.

It also says where the person's word is actually needed. Paul's layer (§2.4 add) holds every design's builds for his Go. Most designs are routine, and the ones that need him are the far-reaching ones: those whose builds change roles, briefs, the spec or the glossary that every session reads. Today no repo can say which those are, so the hold is all or nothing.

Paul approved filing this on 2026-11-16 (his word *file*, in session `led-tds`). Its siblings are filed separately: ledgerboard TD-132, and two ShiftLead entries, one of them making ShiftLead's techlead the judge.

**Location:** files/docs/cadence.md §4.8 (Design review rounds: the stop rule, the cap, the end-of-rounds item); files/docs/procedures/cadence.md step 3; files/scripts/review_stats.py; files/scripts/check_cadence.py row_person(); files/scripts/cadence_config.py; files/docs/cadence.toml; docs/plans/2026-10-30-design-review-rounds.md; files/docs/cadence-changes.md

**Ask:** Write this design in a session with you and agree its shape before its review rounds run?

**Fix:** A design (it changes §4.8 for every consumer). The asker's shape, for the design round to test:

1. **Guidance for ending a design's rounds**, read from the review record:
   - *One more round*: build-changing findings are shrinking, and some are still unread by a review.
   - *Go*: no build-changing fix is unread; only wording is left.
   - *Hold, and split or narrow*: findings are flat or growing, or the same concern recurs.
   - *Hold, and raise*: a question only the person can settle (scope, product, cost).
2. **The judge is not the designer**: a seat independent of the design, such as a techlead (ShiftLead's half is its own entry).
3. **The inputs are counted, not felt**: build-changing findings per round, build-changing fixes still unread, and a concern recurring (the same finding reopened). The review record (`docs/reviews/TD-<n>.md`, `files/scripts/review_stats.py`) counts them.
4. **Reach**: the designer marks each design *routine* or *far-reaching*, with a reason.
5. **`[far-reaching]` in `docs/cadence.toml`**: a repo says what is far-reaching as globs, as `[precheck-paths]` does (`files/scripts/cadence_config.py`):

       [far-reaching]
       paths = ["docs/briefs/**", "src/shiftlead/roles/**", "docs/glossary.md"]

   (ShiftLead's, say; ledgerboard's `["SPEC.md", "ledgerboard/**"]`). A design whose builds name a matching path is far-reaching; any doubt counts as far-reaching; with none set, nothing is.
6. **A backstop at merge**: the cadence check holds a build PR that touches a far-reaching path, under a design whose Go was not the person's, for the person, the way its `person` row (`row_person()` in `files/scripts/check_cadence.py`) holds the person's paths.
7. **The card shows the evidence**: the end-of-rounds item shows the findings trend and the split between build-changing and wording, so the person sees what the judge saw.

Open for the design to settle: how this meets TD-243 (no round ends while a build-changing fix is unread) and TD-249 (the synopsis the item links), both open on the same section; whether *Reach* is a ledger field or a line in the plan; the seed `files/docs/cadence.toml`'s comment; the procedure's wording (`files/docs/procedures/cadence.md` step 3); the ADR (`docs/plans/2026-10-30-design-review-rounds.md` is decided, so a new ADR or a superseding clause, §7.5); and a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

Not part of this entry: Paul's own layer (§2.4 add, builds wait for his Go) becomes *his Go on far-reaching designs*. That edit is his.

**Done when:** cadence.md §4.8 carries the guidance, the Reach mark and `[far-reaching]`; the review record counts what the guidance reads; the end-of-rounds item shows the trend; the cadence check's merge-time backstop is built and tested; the decision is recorded as §7.5 asks; cadence-changes.md has the line.
