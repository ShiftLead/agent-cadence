## TD-284: The cadence teaches a question to the person with its context in later paragraphs, which ledgerboard's next release refuses

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-16 (ledgerboard's designer ao-ledgerboard-lb-designer, through an ask; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** dad9243985cf44ffac63c86087a82817
**Blocked by:** TD-283

**Why:** ledgerboard's design for an entry's body text (its TD-124, plan `docs/plans/TD-124-body-grammar.md` in ShiftLead/ledgerboard, D3) makes `**Ask:**` a one-line data field: the question alone, with whatever context it needs in a prose `**Context:**` section right after it. From the minor release TD-283 vendors, a multi-paragraph Ask is flagged `field-lines`, and the ledger writer refuses an edit that makes a new one.

This repo teaches the opposite today. cadence.md §2.3 says an Ask's first paragraph is the question, *then a blank line and its context in later paragraphs*, and `ledger.py new` and `set` take an `--ask FILE` whose file is written whole as the Ask. So once TD-283 lands, a session following the cadence writes an Ask the writer refuses, and has no flag to put its context where the format wants it.

Asked for by ledgerboard's designer (ao-ledgerboard-lb-designer, by `ask`, 2026-11-17, m-727e339b18e0, about its TD-132).

**Location:** files/scripts/ledger.py (new, set: --ask); files/docs/cadence.md §2.3, §2.14; files/docs/procedures/; files/.claude/skills/; files/docs/cadence-changes.md; tests/; ShiftLead/ledgerboard docs/plans/TD-124-body-grammar.md D3, D5

**Fix:** After TD-283 vendors the release:

1. `ledger.py new` and `set` take `--context FILE` (or `-` for stdin), passed to ledgerboard's writer to write the `**Context:**` section after the Ask; `--ask` takes the one-line question. A test each.
2. cadence.md §2.3 (the Ask rule) and §2.14 (where it says to give an entry its gate and Ask in one write) say: the Ask is one line; its context goes in `**Context:**` through `--context`.
3. Every other place here that hands a session `--ask <file>` follows suit: grep `files/` for `--ask` and for *Ask* in `files/docs/procedures/` (cadence, attention, memory-harvest, stranded-work name it today) and in the skills.
4. A `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

Existing multi-paragraph Asks in this ledger are TD-283's `migrate-body` run, if the release's migration moves them; if it does not, this entry moves them with `ledger.py edit`.

**Done when:** `ledger.py new` and `set` take `--context` with a test each; cadence.md §2.3 and §2.14 and every procedure or skill here that writes an Ask teach a one-line Ask with a **Context:** section; cadence-changes.md has the line; and `ledger.py --check` shows no `field-lines` flag on an Ask in this ledger.
