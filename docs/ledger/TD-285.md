## TD-285: After the next ledgerboard release, a held entry still cannot offer the person its own answers here

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17 (ledgerboard's grinder ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder, through an ask; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** d8ac0fcde7f04ff29f5cf7a986d5f9d7
**Blocked by:** TD-283

**Why:** When an entry waits on a person's decision and no board item asks for it, the gate sweep files a `decide` item offering only *Go | Not yet*, whatever the entry's Ask asks. ledgerboard v3.3 (its TD-119, built as TD-139, ShiftLead/ledgerboard#412 at 46171e4; untagged on 2026-11-17, the tag is Paul's) gives an entry two one-line body fields, `**Answers:**` and `**Lifts:**`, which the sweep then offers in place of *Go | Not yet*, and a `check()` flag, `answers-unusable`, that the ledger writer refuses on an edit that makes it.

This repo's sweep needs no code for it: `files/scripts/gate_sweep.py` hands `sweep()`'s `answers`, `default` and `lifts` to the board writer unchanged (ledgerboard's plan `docs/plans/TD-119-entry-answers.md`, D6 *The consumers*). What is missing here is the way to write them and the rule that says to. cadence.md §2.3 and §2.14 teach a held entry's `**Ask:**` and say nothing of its answers, and `ledger.py new` takes `--ask` but no answers or lifts, so the one write §2.14 asks for (gate and Ask together) cannot carry them. So after the vendor, a session whose Ask asks for *Works* or *One more round* still gets an item offering *Go | Not yet*, and a *Works* typed as a reply does not lift the gate.

Asked for by ledgerboard's grinder (ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder, by `ao td add --repo`, 2026-11-17, m-e2e54e95013e).

**Location:** files/scripts/ledger.py (new, set); files/scripts/gate_sweep.py; files/docs/cadence.md §2.3, §2.14; files/docs/cadence-changes.md; tests/; ShiftLead/ledgerboard docs/plans/TD-119-entry-answers.md D1, D6

**Fix:** After TD-283 vendors v3.3:

1. `ledger.py new` and `set` take `--answers "<a | b (default) | c>"` and `--lifts "<a | b>"`, passed to ledgerboard's writer as the one-line `**Answers:**` and `**Lifts:**` sections; the writer's `answers-unusable` refusal reaches the caller as its other refusals do. A test each, including a refused unusable value. (`ledger.py edit --section Answers --from` may already write one; check, and say so in the test.)
2. A test that the gate sweep's plan for a gated entry carrying both sections offers its answers and lifts, not *Go | Not yet* (the pass-through D6 relies on, held so it stays).
3. cadence.md §2.3 (the Ask) and §2.14 (the one write of gate and Ask) say: an Ask that asks for answers other than Go carries them in `**Answers:**`, and which of them lift in `**Lifts:**` (with none, Go lifts).
4. A `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

Build after TD-284 where both touch `ledger.py new`/`set` and §2.3, or in the same PR.

**Done when:** `ledger.py new` and `set` take `--answers` and `--lifts` with tests; a test holds the sweep offering an entry's own answers; cadence.md §2.3 and §2.14 teach the two fields; cadence-changes.md has the line.
