## TD-291: After you choose one more review round, the design still asks the question you just answered, so it can be built mid-round

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17 (Paul, through his session ao-led-tds-ledger; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Owner:** person
**Status:** Open
**Id take:** e1fd51349b044053a37e13399b585b92
**Blocked by:** decision (Paul)

**Why:** When the person answers `One more round` on a design's end-of-rounds item, the item closes, but the design's entry keeps `**Blocked by:** decision (<person>)` and its `**Ask:**`, which still asks the question just answered. cadence.md §4.8 says only that `One more round` *runs one more, past the cap if need be, then the item again*; unlike `Hold: <note>`, it says nothing about the entry's Ask or its hold while that round runs, and filing the round (where a repo runs it under an entry of its own) adds nothing to the hold.

So for the whole of the bought round the design reads as a live, unasked decision. The gate sweep (§2.14) and ShiftLead's Queue see a `decision (<person>)` gate with an Ask and no open item, and show it as a decide card; a `Go` or a Lift pressed there lifts the design and its builds mid-round, before the round's reviews have read the fixes the person bought the round for.

**Ask:** Should a session change the cadence so a design stays held by its running review round after you choose one more round?

**Fix:** The asker's shape; the build settles the wording.

1. cadence.md §4.8, the `One more round` answer: filing the bought round adds that round's entry to the design's hold, `**Blocked by:** TD-<round>, decision (<person>)`, in the same write that files the round, so no reader takes the design for a live decision while the round runs. Where a repo runs the round without an entry of its own, the design's Ask is rewritten instead to say the round is running (decide which while building, and say it in §4.8).
2. The round's end, when it writes the new end-of-rounds item and the design's new `**Ask:**`, drops `TD-<round>` from the design's `**Blocked by:**` in that same write, leaving `decision (<person>)`.
3. Check whether the gate sweep (§2.14, `files/scripts/gate_sweep.py`) or the cadence check needs to treat a design held by both its round and a decision as not asking yet; if the vendored ledgerboard already reads an open entry in the gate that way, a test pins it.
4. The procedure (`files/docs/procedures/cadence.md` step 3) says the same, and a `files/docs/cadence-changes.md` line.

Check TD-243 and TD-281 first, which also change how §4.8's rounds end, so the three do not write conflicting rules.

**Done when:** §4.8 says what a bought round does to the design's hold and its Ask while it runs and at its end, the procedure matches, and a design whose bought round is running is not offered to the person as a live decision.
