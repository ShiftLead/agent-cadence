## TD-251: Work met in another repo is filed on that repo's ledger, not parked on this board for the person: cadence.md has no rule for it

**Priority:** low
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-07 (ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-2, through ao td add for shiftlead#TD-1544; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-07
**Id take:** 890f983bf69b4e508f22901fffd04437
**Location:** files/docs/cadence.md §3.7 (Who makes the change) and §3's board rules; files/docs/cadence-changes.md; ShiftLead docs/ledger/TD-1544.md

**Why:** When a session meets work that belongs to another repo, nothing in cadence.md says where that work goes. So a session sometimes parks it on its own repo's board as a to-do for the person ("carry this to X"), even where a session could have handed it to X's ledger directly. The person then carries work between repos by hand, and the work sits on the wrong board, where X's sessions never see it, until they do.

Seen 2026-11-07: a ShiftLead board line from TD-1025 asked Paul to file an entry in dev-cadence (now agent-cadence), which has a question seat a session could have handed it to. It became this repo's TD-250 only when Paul asked for it through his session. ShiftLead filed its own half as shiftlead#TD-1544 (open, 2026-11-07): its briefs and design §4.7 *Entries* are to say that cross-repo work goes to the other repo's seat with `ao td add --repo`, with a board item only where no seat exists. The tool-neutral part holds in every cadence repo: another repo's work is filed on that repo's ledger, not parked on this board for the person. It sits beside §3.7 *Who makes the change*, which already says a change a convention asks of a repo is made by a session in that repo, never pushed from outside it.

Asked by ShiftLead's grinder (`ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-2`) on 2026-11-07.

**Resolved:** 2026-11-07 (PR #187)

**Done when:** cadence.md says that another repo's work is filed on that repo's ledger, through whatever reaches it, and that a board item is written only where nothing does and names why. cadence-changes.md has the line.
