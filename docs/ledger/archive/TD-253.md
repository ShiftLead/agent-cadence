## TD-253: ledger.py reads an until gate against the local date, not the UTC day cadence §2.4 names, so --pickable and --check disagree with the home for hours a day

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-12
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-12
**Id take:** 75e886adbc6148dbb7f0a2f67c187d28

**Why:** `ledger.py` judges an `until` gate against this machine's local date, while cadence.md §2.4 and §2.14 say an `until` gate lifts on the UTC day, and ledgerboard's own default (`files/scripts/ledgerboard/ledger.py` `_today()`) and ShiftLead's home read it as one. `_today()` in `files/scripts/ledger.py` falls back to `date.today()`, and everything that reads a gate takes that fallback: the report runner `report()`, which serves `--pickable` and `--list` and passes `--today str(_today(None))` to ledgerboard's `run_report`; `check()` (`--check`, and so the cadence check's `ledger` row, which calls it); and `pickable()`, the library entry point. The writers (`lift` among them) already pass the UTC day, so the readers and the writers disagree with each other in this one file.

So for the hours between local midnight and UTC midnight, an entry whose `until` is today reads pickable in one reader and blocked in another. On a machine in MST (UTC−7) that is 00:00–07:00 UTC every day. ShiftLead's `test_ledger_derived` failed every precheck in that window, and ShiftLead pinned `TZ=UTC` in its test as a stopgap (its TD-1697). Every consumer runs `ledger.py` from its pin, so each one hits the same split, and a grinder can pick an entry the home still shows as gated (or the reverse).

Asked for by ShiftLead's grinder ao-shiftlead-grinder-ao-2 (2026-11-12, `ao td add`), from ShiftLead TD-1697.

**Location:** files/scripts/ledger.py _today() ~426 and its callers pickable() ~431, check() ~438, the report runner report() ~2049, its --today ~2065; files/docs/cadence.md §2.4, §2.14 (until is the UTC day)

**Resolved:** 2026-11-12 (PR #193)
