## TD-264: Nothing that waits on the person is invisible: an entry with an Ask, or owned by the person, carries a decision gate

**Priority:** high
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-14
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-14
**Id take:** eae6bfe4f8b146a1b205fe5c044aad58

**Why:** Paul often can't follow what sessions write to him. He moves between projects all day, and each session is deep in one problem, so its board items and messages read as shorthand: file names, function names, plan numbers and terms the session coined. His words (2026-11-14): "How do we get our TD descriptions to be more human friendly? I believe I have raised this in other TDs before." He has: ShiftLead TD-1801 ("text formatted for humans") and TD-1816. So this should be one rule every writer follows, checked by a tool, not another one-off.

Two cases from 2026-11-14:
- A grinder's steer on ledgerboard TD-108 reached his Queue as one dense paragraph ("fixtures/grammar/'s item-line start… judged by item_problem()… text 13…"). In his words, it was "probably great for someone who is already tracking what grammar and a line start are - but to me they are gibberish until i know what grammar, what line is being talked about." The techlead's one-line recommendation under it was the clearest part.
- ShiftLead TD-1648's decide was well laid out, but its answers didn't say who acts. Does "Pinned, run it again" mean Paul edits his profile file himself, or a session does it on his answer?

cadence.md §3.3 already says to write for someone outside the work (TD-084, *An item explains itself, in plain English*). It is prose that no tool checks, and these items passed it. `board_edit.py add` is the one writer every repo's sessions use for a board item (TD-206), so a check there reaches all of them.

Asked for by a session of Paul's in ShiftLead/ledgerboard (ao-led-tds-ledger, three notes and an `ask`, 2026-11-14: m-6d7f329d3a51, m-8b05b8ab9151, m-522ca5fb0d17, m-5af1d1c51e0d). ShiftLead is asked separately for the Queue's side.

**Location:** files/scripts/board_edit.py (add: passthrough() ~223); files/docs/cadence.md §3.3 (TD-084's *An item explains itself, in plain English*; TD-122's *leads with one sentence*); files/docs/cadence-changes.md; tests/

**Resolved:** 2026-11-14 (PR #212)

**Done when:** An open entry with an `**Ask:**`, or an Owner who is the person, but no `decision (<person>)` gate is refused by `ledger.py new` and `set` and fails the cadence check's `ledger` row (a test holds each). The six entries named in rule 6 are each gated, or their Log says why they do not wait on the person. Rules 1–4 and 7 are TD-268's Done when.
