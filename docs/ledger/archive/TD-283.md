## TD-283: This repo's ledger cannot be moved to ledgerboard's stricter entry text until it takes the release that flags and migrates it

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** feature
**Added:** 2026-11-16 (ledgerboard's designer ao-ledgerboard-lb-designer, through an ask; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-18
**Id take:** 688129286c544932bde1b13d9869739d

**Why:** ledgerboard's next minor release (its TD-133, filed by ShiftLead/ledgerboard#402, still open on 2026-11-16; the first of TD-124's two releases; plan `docs/plans/TD-124-body-grammar.md` in ShiftLead/ledgerboard, D7, Paul's choice of two steps on 2026-11-16) gives `check()` four flags on an entry's body: `lead-form`, `glued-lead`, `fence-open` and `field-lines`. Its writer refuses an edit that makes one, and it adds a `migrate-body` that rewrites a ledger, open and archived entries alike, so that none is flagged. The major release after it reads a body strictly, and a ledger not migrated by then reads wrong.

This repo reaches ledgerboard only through its vendored copy (`files/scripts/ledgerboard/`, `vendor_ledgerboard.sh`) and the `ledger.py` wrapper, and code here never calls ledgerboard's writers directly (cadence.md §2.12). So until this repo vendors the minor, passes `migrate-body` through `ledger.py` and runs it on its own ledger, no consumer pinned to agent-cadence can migrate before the major arrives.

Asked for by ledgerboard's designer (ao-ledgerboard-lb-designer, by `ask`, 2026-11-17, m-61bb27fb9805).
