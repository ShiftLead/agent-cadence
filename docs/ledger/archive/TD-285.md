## TD-285: After the next ledgerboard release, a held entry still cannot offer the person its own answers here

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt
**Added:** 2026-11-17 (ledgerboard's grinder ao-ledgerboard-lb-grinder, through an ask; drafted by ao-agent-cadence-ac-techlead)
**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-18
**Id take:** d8ac0fcde7f04ff29f5cf7a986d5f9d7
**Blocked by:** TD-283

**Why:** When an entry waits on a person's decision and no board item asks for it, the gate sweep files a `decide` item offering only *Go | Not yet*, whatever the entry's Ask asks. ledgerboard v3.3 (its TD-119, built as TD-139, ShiftLead/ledgerboard#412 at 46171e4; untagged on 2026-11-17, the tag is Paul's) gives an entry two one-line body fields, `**Answers:**` and `**Lifts:**`, which the sweep then offers in place of *Go | Not yet*, and a `check()` flag, `answers-unusable`, that the ledger writer refuses on an edit that makes it.

This repo's sweep needs no code for it: `files/scripts/gate_sweep.py` hands `sweep()`'s `answers`, `default` and `lifts` to the board writer unchanged (ledgerboard's plan `docs/plans/TD-119-entry-answers.md`, D6 *The consumers*). What is missing here is the rule that says to write them, and a way to write them in one go. cadence.md §2.3 names an `**Answers:**` line only as a sign that an entry waits on the person and as what `edit --section Answers` checks; neither it nor §2.14 says the sweep offers those answers, or that `**Lifts:**` names which of them lift. `ledger.py new` and `set` take `--ask` but no answers or lifts, so the one write §2.14 asks for (gate and Ask together) cannot carry them, and `edit --section Lifts` runs no check of its own. So after the vendor, a session whose Ask asks for *Works* or *One more round* still gets an item offering *Go | Not yet*, and a *Works* typed as a reply does not lift the gate.
