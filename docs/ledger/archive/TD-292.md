## TD-292: After the next ledgerboard release, a seat answering for the person here is neither possible nor guarded

**Priority:** medium
**Type:** debt

**Status:** Resolved 2026-11-18

**Why:** ledgerboard's next minor release, v3.4 (its TD-132, design `docs/plans/TD-132-delegated-answers.md` in ShiftLead/ledgerboard, built as its TD-146; Paul's Go on 2026-11-17, ShiftLead/ledgerboard#434), lets a delegate answer a board item for the person and say so: `**Decided:** <date> by <delegate> for <person> (<scope>) — <label>`. A board's `README.md` declares who may, in a `Delegates:` line (`director for paul (merge-line)`), and an item a delegate may answer carries `**Scope:** <scope>`. The package's writer and merge-gate verdicts accept a delegated answer only where a faultless grant covers the item's scope. ShiftLead's director seat is the first delegate: it is to answer some cards for the person, and a delegated answer must say it was delegated, or neither later reviews nor the person can tell their own decisions from the seat's.

The package compares names and nothing more; three things it leaves to each consumer (its D3 *limit*, D4, D6), and this repo does none of them yet:

- `board_edit.py` cannot write a delegated answer or a scoped item: it passes `board.py`'s flags through, but only once the vendored copy has `--delegate` and `--scope`.
- Who may change a grant is the consumer's person-paths gate. Here the person row (TD-230) lists only `docs/ledger/log-sources.toml`, so a session's reviewed PR could add a `Delegates:` grant, and so widen what a seat may answer for Paul, with no hold for him.
- The package cannot tell who raised an item. A delegate whose PR adds an item with a `Scope` and then answers it gives itself the authority; so the board-write carve-outs here must let no such item in without review.

Until this lands, ShiftLead's director seat cannot write a delegated answer on this repo's board (ShiftLead holds that until every reading repo has v3.4), and a consumer pinned to agent-cadence has no guard on its own grants.
